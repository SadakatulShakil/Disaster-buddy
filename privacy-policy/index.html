Privacy Policy

TUKU (Disaster Buddy)

Disaster Buddy
Effective date: 5 November 2026  ·  Developer: Xpreso Lab

Made for children · ages 5+

TUKU (Disaster Buddy) is an educational app that teaches children how to stay safe during disasters such as earthquakes, floods, and lightning. We built this app for children, and protecting their privacy is our highest priority. This policy explains, in plain language, how the app handles information.

The short version

Currently, we do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information from anyone who uses this app — children or adults. The app works entirely on your device and does not require an account, a login, or an internet connection to learn.

Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect, request, or have access to any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses
  • Dates of birth, age, gender, or photographs
  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts, microphone, or camera data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioural tracking data

Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information only on your own device so your child's learning can continue where it left off. This includes things like which lessons and activities are completed, badges and stickers earned, daily-challenge streaks, and app settings such as language and sound. This information:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not visible to us or to anyone else,
  • is not linked to any identity, and
  • can be erased at any time using the reset options in the Parent area, or by uninstalling the app.

No accounts · No tracking

  • The app does not require or offer any account or sign-in.
  • The app does not use third-party analytics or tracking services.
  • The app does not sell or share any data, because it collects none.

Advertising (possible future feature)

The app currently shows no advertisements. We may introduce limited, minimal advertising in a future version. If we ever do, we commit to the following, in line with the Google Play Families policy for children's apps:

  • we will use only a Google-certified advertising provider approved for children's / family apps;
  • we will show only non-personalised (contextual) ads — never ads based on tracking or profiling a child;
  • we will not use behavioural advertising, remarketing, or advertising identifiers to target children;
  • we will keep ads minimal and non-disruptive; and
  • we will update this Privacy Policy and our Google Play Data safety disclosures before any ads appear, so this page always reflects what the app actually does.

Until this section is updated with an effective date for advertising, the app contains no ads and collects no advertising data.

Internet use

The core learning experience works fully offline. If an internet connection is used, it is only to let a parent open official government safety resources or, where available, download optional content. The app does not send any personal information when it does this.

Links and features for parents

Some features intended for adults — such as links to official emergency and weather resources, or an emergency phone number — are placed behind a parental gate. Opening an external link or dialer hands you over to that app or website, which has its own privacy practices that we do not control. We encourage parents to review them.

Children's privacy

This app is designed to comply with children's privacy standards, including the Google Play Families policy and the principles of COPPA and GDPR-K. Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing about a child that we could store, share, or misuse. Should advertising be added in the future, it will follow the child-safe conditions described above.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the “Effective date” at the top and post the new version at this same web address.
